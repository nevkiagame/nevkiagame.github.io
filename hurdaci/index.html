Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?

Your coins, records, unlocked cars and looks, collection, business and daily rewards are saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The whole game works without internet. Only the optional videos (continue a run or double your earnings) need a connection.

How do I unlock new cities?

Each city opens when you reach the required tower height in the previous one. Open the map from the main menu to see what you need.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game, go to Settings → Privacy / Ad consent (shown in regions where consent is required). On iPhone and iPad you can also use Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our privacy policy for details.

How do I turn off reminders?

Go to Settings → Notifications in the game, or turn off notifications for Hurdacı in your device settings.
