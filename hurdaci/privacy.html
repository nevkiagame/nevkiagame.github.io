Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026. Türkçe metin aşağıda.

This policy explains how the mobile game Hurdacı: Car Stack Tower (the "Game"), developed by Nevkia Games ("we", "us"), handles information. If anything is unclear, contact us at nevkiagame@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. We do not run servers for the Game and the Game has no in-app purchases.

Your progress and settings (coins, records, unlocked cars and looks, collection, business, daily rewards and missions, and sound, language and notification settings) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. Some ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch to continue a run or double your earnings. After some runs, a full-screen ad may also appear between runs; there are no ads during gameplay. To show and measure ads, the AdMob software in the Game may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking on iOS);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads (for example views and taps) and diagnostic data such as crash information related to ads.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have given consent, show personalized ads. Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Notifications and sharing

The Game can show local reminders (for example when your daily reward is ready or your in-game business safe is full). They are scheduled on your device; no notification data is sent to us. The Game asks for permission first, and you can turn reminders off in the Game under Settings → Notifications or in your device settings.

If you use the Share button, your device's share sheet (or clipboard) is used to share your score with an app you choose. We do not receive what you share.

4. Your choices and consent

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

When you first open the Game, a consent message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform asks whether your data may be used for personalized advertising. You can change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings → Privacy / Ad consent. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads.

iOS (iPhone and iPad)

The Game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not shared. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (on most devices Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for teens. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not store any of your personal data on our own servers. Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google's privacy controls at My Ad Center.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Turkish KVKK or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through the settings above or through Google. For anything else, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Nevkia Games · nevkiagame@gmail.com

Bildirimler ve paylaşım

Oyun, günlük ödülünüz hazır olduğunda ya da oyundaki işletmenizin kasası dolduğunda yerel hatırlatmalar gösterebilir. Bunlar cihazınızda planlanır, bize veri gönderilmez. Oyun önce izin ister; hatırlatmaları oyunda Ayarlar → Bildirim bölümünden ya da cihaz ayarlarından kapatabilirsiniz.
