Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?

Your stars, best times, coins and endless-mode record are saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

How does endless mode work?

Every run starts fresh with three lives. Each fall costs a life. When you run out, you can watch an optional video to get one more life and continue from your last checkpoint, or start again.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

On iPhone and iPad, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See our privacy policy for details.
