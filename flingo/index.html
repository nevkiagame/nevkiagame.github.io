Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

I bought "Remove Ads" but I see ads again.

Open Settings → Restore purchases in the game, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy settings in the game (shown where required by law), or see our privacy policy.

Which languages are available?

English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French. You can switch in Settings → Language.
