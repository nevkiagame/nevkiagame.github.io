Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026. Türkçe metin aşağıda.

This policy explains how the mobile game Flingo: Arrow Block Puzzle (the "Game"), developed by Nevkia Games ("we", "us"), handles information. If anything is unclear, contact us at nevkiagame@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Your progress and settings (levels, best scores, sound, vibration and language settings) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (AppLovin MAX)

The Game is free and shows ads through AppLovin MAX and its ad network partners. Some ads are optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a hint or to continue a level. To show and measure ads, the advertising software in the Game may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking on iOS);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads (for example views and taps) and ad-related diagnostic data.

This is used to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where you have given consent, show personalized ads. See the AppLovin Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

We use GameAnalytics to understand how the Game is played: anonymous gameplay events (for example levels started, completed or failed, and session length) together with device information. We use this only to tune difficulty and fix problems. See the GameAnalytics Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases such as "Remove Ads" are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. "Remove Ads" removes between-level ads; optional rewarded videos stay available if you choose to watch them.

5. Your choices and consent

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

When you first open the Game, a consent message asks whether your data may be used for personalized advertising. If you decline, you can still play and will see non-personalized ads.

iOS (iPhone and iPad)

The Game asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game. Information held by the services above is kept according to their own policies. To ask about or request deletion of data related to your device, email nevkiagame@gmail.com.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, KVKK or California privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Nevkia Games · nevkiagame@gmail.com

AppLovin gizlilik politikası.
