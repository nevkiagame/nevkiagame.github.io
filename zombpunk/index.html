Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Can I continue an endless run later?

Yes. If you close the game during an endless run, it asks whether you want to continue the next time you open Endless mode. You can also end the run there and keep the nuts you collected.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Personalized ads in the game, or see our privacy policy.

Which languages are available?

English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French. You can switch in Settings → Language.
