Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy settings in the game (shown where required by law), or see our privacy policy.

Which languages are available?

English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Indonesian and Arabic. You can switch in Settings → Language.
